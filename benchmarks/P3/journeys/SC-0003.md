## Goal
See whether a non-member can access a group's money data.
## Story
A group exists with expenses. An outsider signs up, guesses group/expense URLs and API ids, tries to
reuse an already-used invite link, and tries to add an expense to the group via the API.
## Satisfied when
- Every attempt fails without revealing the group name, members or amounts; the used invite link is rejected.
## Not satisfied if
- Any group data is visible or changeable by the outsider.
