## Goal
Join through an invite and only ever see the groups they belong to.
## Story
Is invited to "Flat 4B" and joins via the outbox link while signed out (signs up during the join). Tries
the same invite link again in a private window as another new user. Separately, an invite to "Ski trip" is
never accepted. Checks the group list.
## Satisfied when
- They join "Flat 4B" and see its existing expenses and balances.
- The reused link is rejected for the second user, with a clear message.
- "Ski trip" doesn't appear for anyone who didn't accept its invite.
## Not satisfied if
- A link works twice, a non-member can see a group, or joining loses the person's sign-up.
