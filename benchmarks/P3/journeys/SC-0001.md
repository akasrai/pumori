## Goal
Set up a trip group, invite two friends, log shared costs and see who owes what.
## Story
Ana creates "Pokhara trip" in NPR, invites Bikash and Chen (they join via the outbox links).
Ana pays 9,000.00 for the hotel split equally among all three. Bikash pays 1,500.50 for dinner split
equally between Bikash and Chen. Chen checks balances.
## Satisfied when
- Balances: Ana +6,000.00; Bikash -2,249.75; Chen -3,750.25 (equal split rounding to payer is fine
  only if totals still sum to zero).
- "Who pays whom" lists at most 2 transfers that settle everyone.
## Not satisfied if
- Balances do not sum to zero, amounts are off by more than rounding, or a friend cannot join.
