## Goal
Fix a wrongly entered expense and trust the history.
## Story
A member enters 50.00 instead of 5.00 for coffee, notices, edits it to 5.00, then deletes a duplicate
expense they created. Another member tries to delete an expense they did not create.
## Satisfied when
- Balances reflect only the corrected and remaining expenses; the activity feed shows the edit and the
  delete; the other member cannot delete someone else's expense.
## Not satisfied if
- Balances keep stale amounts, history is missing, or non-creators can delete.
