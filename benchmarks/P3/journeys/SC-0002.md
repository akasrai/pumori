## Goal
Record an exact-amount split, then settle a debt and see balances reach zero.
## Story
Two flatmates in a EUR group. Dana pays 100.00 groceries split exactly 70.00 Dana / 30.00 Eli.
Eli tries an exact split that does not add up (gets an error), fixes it. Eli records paying Dana 30.00.
## Satisfied when
- The bad split is rejected with a clear message; after the settlement both balances are 0.00 and the
  activity feed shows the expense and the settlement.
## Not satisfied if
- Mismatched splits are saved, or balances do not reach zero.
