## Goal
Split awkward amounts and trust that everything adds up to the cent.
## Story
Group of three (USD). Pays 100.00 split equally among all three. Another member pays 10.01 split equally
between two members. A third pays 0.03 split equally among three. Then one member records settling up
exactly what the "who pays whom" list tells them.
## Satisfied when
- Every split's shares add up exactly to its amount (no lost or invented cents); remainders go to the payer.
- Balances always sum to 0.00.
- After the suggested settlement is recorded, that member's balance is 0.00 and the list updates.
## Not satisfied if
- Any cent is lost or duplicated, balances don't sum to zero, or the suggested transfers don't settle debts.
