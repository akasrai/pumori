## Goal
Find out whether they can see or change someone else's to-dos.
## Story
User A signs up and adds "Secret surprise party plan". User B signs up in another session, looks at
their own list, then tries to reach A's to-do by guessing/altering URLs or replaying API requests with
different ids (read, edit, delete).
## Satisfied when
- B's list is empty; B never sees A's title anywhere; every attempt on A's item fails without revealing it.
- A's to-do is unchanged afterwards.
## Not satisfied if
- Any of A's data is visible to B, or B can change/delete it, or errors leak internal details.
