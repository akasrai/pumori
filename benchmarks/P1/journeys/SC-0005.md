## Goal
Manage a long list without losing track: order and the open counter must stay right.
## Story
Adds 30 to-dos quickly (numbered "Task 01" … "Task 30"). Marks Task 03, Task 17 and Task 29 done, then
marks Task 17 open again. Edits "Task 10" to "Task 10 — call the bank". Deletes Task 22. Reloads the page.
## Satisfied when
- After reload: 29 to-dos remain (30 − 1 deleted); the counter shows 27 open (29 − 2 done).
- Open to-dos come first, newest first (Task 30 at the top), and Task 17 is back among the open ones.
  Completed Task 03 and Task 29 are below all open to-dos.
- The edited title shows exactly, including the dash.
## Not satisfied if
- The counter or order is wrong after any step or after reload, or an action changes the wrong to-do.
