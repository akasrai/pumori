## Goal
Add to-dos quickly without the app accepting garbage.
## Story
Tries to add an empty to-do, then one of only spaces, then one 250 characters long, then a normal one
containing <b>bold?</b> as literal text.
## Satisfied when
- Empty, whitespace-only and over-long titles are rejected with a clear message and nothing is saved.
- The literal "<b>bold?</b>" text is shown as typed (not rendered as HTML).
## Not satisfied if
- Invalid items are saved, errors are unclear, or the markup is interpreted.
