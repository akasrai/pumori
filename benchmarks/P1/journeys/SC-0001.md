## Goal
Get three things out of their head and onto a list, then tick one off.
## Story
Signs up with a new email. Adds "Buy milk", "Call grandma 📞" and "Book dentist for Mia (before Friday!)".
Realises the milk is already bought and marks it done. Glances at how many things are left.
## Satisfied when
- All three to-dos appear with titles exactly as typed (emoji and punctuation intact).
- "Buy milk" shows as completed and sits below the open ones.
- The open counter shows 2.
## Not satisfied if
- They have to reload to see changes, or any title is truncated or mangled.
