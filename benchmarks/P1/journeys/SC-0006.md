## Goal
Use the list in two browser tabs without corrupting it or seeing a broken screen.
## Story
Signs in and opens the list in tab A and tab B. In tab A adds "Pick up parcel". In tab B (not yet
reloaded) deletes nothing but marks an existing to-do "Water plants" done. In tab A deletes "Water plants".
In tab B, without reloading, tries to edit "Water plants" again. Then reloads both tabs.
## Satisfied when
- The stale action in tab B fails gracefully (a clear message or the item disappears), with no crash, blank
  screen or raw error.
- After reloading, both tabs show the same list: "Pick up parcel" present, "Water plants" gone, and the
  counter correct.
## Not satisfied if
- The deleted item comes back, data differs between tabs after reload, or the UI breaks.
