## Goal
Keep the important note on top and clear old clutter without losing it.
## Story
Creates 3 notes, pins "Passwords hint (not the passwords!)", archives "Old trip plan", opens Archive,
restores it, then edits another note so it becomes most recent.
## Satisfied when
- The pinned note stays first even after another note is edited.
- The archived note disappears from the main list, appears in Archive, and returns after restore.
## Not satisfied if
- Pinned order breaks, or archiving loses content.
