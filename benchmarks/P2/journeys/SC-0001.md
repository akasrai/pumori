## Goal
Capture several ideas with tags and find one again later by search and by tag.
## Story
Signs up. Creates 4 notes: "Café pricing idea" (tags: business, coffee), "Reading list" (tags: books),
"Espresso grind notes" with a multi-line body mentioning "crema" (tags: coffee), "Grocery run" (no tags).
Searches "CREMA", then filters by tag coffee.
## Satisfied when
- Search for "CREMA" finds only "Espresso grind notes".
- Filter coffee shows exactly the two coffee notes. Line breaks in the body are preserved.
## Not satisfied if
- Search is case-sensitive, results include other notes, or tags are lost after saving.
