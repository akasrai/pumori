## Goal
Find yesterday's notes intact after logging out and in.
## Story
Creates a note with a long body (several paragraphs), tags it "draft", logs out, logs in, opens it,
adds a paragraph, removes the tag.
## Satisfied when
- The body is complete with paragraphs; the edit persists; the tag is removed and "draft" disappears
  from the filter list if no other note uses it.
## Not satisfied if
- Content is truncated or edits are lost.
